Setting up the stand

about 1 min

What you need, and how to arrange it

  • You need: a stacking wire rack, white A4 copy paper, and a light
  • Fix the A4 paper in place so it does not move
  • Place the iPhone where the rack's grid does not appear in the frame
  • If shadows get in the way, use “Light On” in the app
2

Measuring on a stand

about 1 min 30 s

Measure up, down, left and right in one go

  • Zoom in or out so the endoscope fills the screen
  • Adjust the endoscope until the guideline turns green
  • Switch the direction tab and capture and save each of the four directions
3

Measuring handheld

about 1 min 50 s

How to measure when you have no stand

  • Turn on “Handheld mode” in Settings to show the level guide
  • Hold the iPhone level until the level guide turns green
  • When your hands are full, you can operate the app by voice (“up”, “capture”, “save” and so on)

Shooting tips

Shoot on plain white paper

Wood-grain desks, non-woven cloth and angle measuring plates have patterns the app can mistake for the endoscope. Plain A4 copy paper is all you need.

Avoid shadows

A dark shadow can be hard to tell apart from the endoscope. Light the area evenly, and if a shadow remains, tap “Light On” at the bottom right of the screen.

Get the size and alignment right

Zoom in until the tip and the base fit inside the corner marks, and line the insertion tube up with the vertical line on screen. This makes the reading more accurate.

Choose the direction before you shoot

Select the up, down, left or right tab before you take the photo. If you switch it afterwards, the measurement is discarded and the app shows “Direction changed. Please take the photo again.”

Retake as often as you like

However many times you shoot the same endoscope in the same direction on the same day, only the most recent one is kept. If a photo does not come out well, just take it again.

You can use your voice

Say “up”, “down”, “left” or “right” to choose the direction, and “zoom in”, “zoom out”, “capture” or “save” to carry on. It works on a stand or handheld, and responds whenever the Measure screen is open.
