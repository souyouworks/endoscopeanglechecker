souyou works (“we” or “us”) sets out this Privacy Policy (“this Policy”) concerning the handling of user information in the iOS application “EndoScopeAngleChecker” (“the App”) that we provide. We comply with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information of Japan and other applicable laws and guidelines, and handle user information appropriately in accordance with this Policy.

Article 1 (Scope)

This Policy applies to all user information handled within the App when users use the App.

If you move from the App to an external website or service, the handling of information on that site or service is governed by the policy of its provider and falls outside the scope of this Policy.

Article 2 (Definitions)

In this Policy, the following terms have the meanings set out below.

  • “User information” means information that users enter into the App, information captured or recorded through the App, and any other information handled within the App.
  • “On the device” means the internal storage of the user's mobile device on which the App is installed.
  • “Personal information” means personal information as defined in Article 2, Paragraph 1 of the Act on the Protection of Personal Information of Japan.

Article 3 (Types of information handled)

The user information handled in the App is as follows.

  • Names of hospitals and other facilities
  • Names of staff members (the persons performing the inspection)
  • Endoscope model information (model name, serial number, management number, etc.)
  • Measurement records (measured angle, date and time of measurement, direction, and pass/fail result)
  • Images of the endoscope captured during measurement

All of the information listed above is stored only on the device. We do not operate servers or any other equipment, and we never collect this information automatically.

Article 4 (Purposes of use)

The App uses the user information set out in the preceding Article only for the following purposes.

  • Calculating and displaying the bending angle of the endoscope
  • Saving, searching and aggregating measurement records, and displaying changes over time
  • Exporting inspection records as reports (PDF or CSV)
  • Transferring data when changing devices, where the user explicitly performs this operation

Article 5 (External transmission and provision to third parties)

We never transmit user information externally without an action by the user. The App contains no third-party modules that send information externally for advertising, analytics or any other purpose.

Communication with external parties occurs only in the cases listed in the table below, and each requires an explicit action by the user.

When communication occursInformation sent and how it is handled
In-app purchase Communication takes place with the servers of Apple Inc. Payment is processed by Apple, and we never obtain credit card details or any other payment information.
Reporting a problem Only when the user chooses to send a report from “Report a Problem” in the App, the content and name entered by the user, device information (model name, OS version and App version), the App's activity log, and any images attached by the user are sent by email to the contact point set out in Article 15. We ask for a name so that we can reply to the report.

In the case above, because the report is sent from the user's own email account, the email address used and the display name set for that account (such as a name) are conveyed to us. These are not collected and sent by the App; they are conveyed as a necessary part of how email works. We use them only to reply to the report and to ask follow-up questions.

Reporting a problem is optional, and the user can review the content before sending. We use information obtained through such reports only to investigate and fix the problem, and, except as required by law, we do not provide it to third parties without the user's prior consent.

Article 6 (Information we receive through the App Store)

In addition to the above, we may receive the following information through the App Store operated by Apple Inc. This information is not sent by the App; it is collected by Apple and provided to us.

  • Records of abnormal terminations of the App (so-called crash reports), limited to cases where the user has agreed to share such information with Apple
  • Download counts, usage and other statistics

The information above is provided in a form that does not identify individuals, and we use it only to improve the quality of the App. Users can control whether this information is shared in iOS Settings (“Analytics & Improvements” under “Privacy & Security”).

Article 7 (Use of device functions and permissions)

The App uses the following device functions only for the purposes stated.

Article 8 (Speech recognition processing)

All speech recognition in the App is processed entirely on the device. Audio captured through the microphone is never sent to external servers, and no recordings are stored.

Article 9 (Exporting and sharing data)

Measurement records (images, CSV and PDF) and the transfer data used when changing devices are sent to the destination chosen by the user (email, AirDrop, etc.) only when the user performs an export. The choice of destination and the sending are carried out by the user's own action and at the user's own responsibility.

Article 10 (Security measures and storage)

Because data in the App is stored only on the device, its security depends on the user's management of the device, such as setting a passcode and biometric authentication. As a supplementary measure, the App offers protection by biometric authentication at launch.

Data in the App may be included in device backups made with standard iOS features (iCloud Backup, or backups via iTunes or the Finder). Whether such backups are enabled is managed by the user in iOS Settings.

Information we receive through problem reports under Article 5 is kept in an email account that we manage, and we take the necessary measures, including enabling two-factor authentication on that account.

Article 11 (Use of external services and storage outside Japan)

We use an email service provided by Google LLC to receive problem reports under Article 5. Accordingly, when a user sends a problem report, its content is stored on servers managed by Google (which may be located outside Japan).

We use this service after reviewing the policies on personal information protection and the security measures published by Google.

By contrast, the user information set out in Article 3 (including measurement records and images) is stored only on the device and is never transferred to any other country.

Article 12 (Retention period)

Information we receive through problem reports under Article 5 is deleted without delay once the investigation and fix of the problem are complete. However, where it is necessary to confirm whether a similar problem recurs, we may keep it only for as long as necessary.

Notwithstanding the preceding paragraph, if a user requests deletion, we will delete the information promptly as set out in Article 13.

Article 13 (Deleting data)

  • Measurement records can be deleted within the App (for example, by swiping to delete in History).
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on the device.
  • We do not hold user information in normal use. As a rule, therefore, there is no need to ask us to delete anything.
  • However, if you have sent a problem report under Article 5, its content is kept in our email. In that case, if you would like it deleted, please contact the contact point in Article 15 and we will delete it promptly.

Article 14 (Requests for disclosure, etc., and use by minors)

Users may, in accordance with applicable law, request disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use, or suspension of provision to third parties of their own personal information held by us. Please send such requests to the contact point in Article 15. Note that the only information we hold is that relating to problem reports as described in Article 13.

If the user is a minor, the user must obtain the consent of a parent or other legal guardian before using the App.

Article 16 (Changes to this Policy)

We may change this Policy in response to changes in law, changes to the App's features, or other reasons. When we make important changes, we will notify users in advance through the App or its App Store listing.

The revised Policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.

Article 17 (Governing law and jurisdiction)

This Policy is governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of Japan.

Any dispute between us and a user concerning this Policy or the App shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction, as the court of first instance, of the court having jurisdiction over our location.
